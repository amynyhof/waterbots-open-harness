# Build update — the dated fact Wellington phrases

**Refreshed at every close-out**, the maintainer's ruling of 23 Sep 2026, a step of the close-out
ritual in [PROCESS_RULES_for_ShellB.md](../../PROCESS_RULES_for_ShellB.md). The desk carries one
line in her words that the site is under build and that a visitor can ask Wellington for a build
update; this file is what he knows when they do. Facts and rules, never lines: he phrases them
himself. Only the region between the markers reaches his prompt, generated by
`scripts/build-prompt-modules.mjs` under the staleness gate; everything outside is for people.

**What goes in the region.** The date; what is built and live; what is being built now; what comes
next, in order; and nothing that is not true on the day it is written. No promise of a date. Where
a thing is not live, the region says so. **Keep it short**: his prompt has a size gate of 24,000
characters in `check-wellington`, and this region shares that room with everything else he holds;
about three hundred characters is the budget today; the pull request that adds a
larger region reports the room left under the gate, and the number is hers to move.
