# Plain words — the method's terms, and the everyday words agents say

**Drafted 14 Nov 2026, the maintainer's word on pull request #173.** One list for every agent on
this site. Each line pairs a term the cards, the worksheet or the record use with the everyday
words a newcomer knows. An agent reads the term and says the everyday words. Only the region
between the markers reaches the prompts, generated by `scripts/build-prompt-modules.mjs` under the
staleness gate; everything outside it is for people.

**Keep it short.** Both prompts sit near their size gates, and this region is in each of them.
A line earns its place when an agent has said the term to a visitor, or would. The words that
fail a measured walk are a separate list, `CARD_WORDS` in `api/_reply.ts`; this list says what
to say instead.

Its partner is [`before-and-after.md`](./before-and-after.md): whole sentences, examples not rules.
