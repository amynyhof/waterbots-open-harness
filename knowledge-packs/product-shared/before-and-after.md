# Before and after — sentences an agent said, and how to say them

**Drafted 14 Nov 2026, the maintainer's word on pull request #173.** Examples, not rules. Each
pair is a sentence an agent said on a walk and the way a person would say it. Agents read them for
the shape of the change, never as lines to copy. Only the region between the markers reaches the
prompts, generated by `scripts/build-prompt-modules.mjs` under the staleness gate.

**Add a pair when a walk finds one.** Every "Not" must fail the measured walk's word list
(`CARD_WORDS` in `api/_reply.ts`) and every "This" must pass it; `scripts/check-reply-guard.mjs`
holds both.

Its partner is [`plain-words.md`](./plain-words.md): one term at a time.

- Not: "you'd write down the four conditions the method lists, with evidence for each".
  This: "Evidence that your program needs extra funding to keep running helps build a strong case for carbon credits."
- Not: "they are a technology the carbon method names".
  This: "they're a technology that often qualifies for carbon credits".
- Not: "That's everything I need for a first look".
  This: "That's all I need from you for now".
