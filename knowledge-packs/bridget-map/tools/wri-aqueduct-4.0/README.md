**Level 6 is as-published.** Those figures are WRI’s.

**Level 4 is derived.** Aqueduct publishes water stress at Level 6 only. The
world view is an area-weighted majority of each Level 4 basin’s Level 6
children, and must say so wherever it renders. Presenting that derived value as
WRI’s is a fabricated claim about someone else’s data.

No stress table and no PDF body text are kept here.
