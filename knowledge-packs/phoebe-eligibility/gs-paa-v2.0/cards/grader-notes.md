## Applies set — moved into `applies-cards-gs.md` on 23 Sep 2026

Graded "passes as redrafted", with one addition on card T4 (the transition-assistance fact). The
notes that sat at the foot of the draft:

These notes are for the maintainer and are moved to `grader-notes.md` before the file is
read by anything.

1. **T4 leans on two documents outside the methodology.** Both pages resolved on
   22 Sep 2026. The methodology itself has no transition clause of its own; the only
   place the V1-to-V2 timetable for this method is written is Annex 5 of the auditor's
   requirements, which is why that document is cited on a card rather than only on the
   README.
2. **The date discrepancy** (9 July on the cover, 7 July in the headers and on the
   publisher's page) is stated once, in the source table, and the cards cite the cover
   date to match Calvin's packs.
3. **T2 and suppressed demand.** The method restricts suppressed demand to micro- and
   small-scale activities (§3.2.4.2). That restriction is an eligibility row (M6 in the
   proposal), not part of the applies test, so T2 names suppressed demand as a baseline
   the method counts without saying which projects may claim it.
4. **The "what kind" field.** Item A16 says Wellington will stop asking it. T1 to T3
   are what replace it on Phoebe's side; nothing on these cards depends on the field.
